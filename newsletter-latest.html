Wholesaling disclosure: put it in writing, every time

Florida doesn’t license wholesalers — but it does punish anyone who markets a property they don’t own. The safe pattern: you market your contract, never the house, and your assignment language and your seller’s written acknowledgement both say so plainly. One paragraph has saved more deals than any negotiation tactic we teach.
