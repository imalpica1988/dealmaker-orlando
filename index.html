A teach‑a‑thon, not a sell‑a‑thon.

Dealmaker Catalyst was built by Jim Ingersoll on one idea: your network is your net worth. Its national events are known for teaching instead of selling, a room where new and experienced investors learn, share and close together.

Dealmaker Orlando brings that same culture to Central Florida. We're a local community of investors who meet monthly to find deals, fund them and grow, with a national network behind us.
